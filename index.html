Questions

Before you call

Where are your branches?

We have two branches, in Mahishadal and Nandakumar. Choose the nearest one when you book, or call it directly.

Do I have to join after my visit?

No. Visit, look around and talk to the trainer. You join only if you like it.

Which plan is the cheapest per month?

The 12-month plan at ₹4,800 works out to ₹400 a month. The 3-month and 6-month plans are both ₹500 a month.

Can I get diet advice?

Yes. The trainer talks through diet with you during your visit.
